Orang Pendek

Information

Type: unknown hominid

Origin: Indonesia

Threat Level: Very low / minimal

Habitat: Forests

Overview

Orang Pendek (Indonesian for "short person") is an elusive, undocumented cryptid purported to inhabit the remote, mountainous rainforests of Sumatra, Indonesia. Witnesses describe it as a bipedal, ape-like creature standing 80 to 150 cm (2.5 to 5 feet) tall, heavily built, and covered in short, dark fur.

Appearance

Described as having immense upper-body strength, long arms, and a mostly human-like walk. Its fur color varies in sightings, typically reported as black, gray, or reddish/honey-colored.

Habitat

Unlike orangutans, which are arboreal, the Orang Pendek is almost exclusively a ground-dwelling creature.

Reported sightings

  • The creature has been documented by local forest tribes, Dutch colonists, and Western travelers for well over a century.

Theories

Some researchers speculate that the Orang Pendek could be a new species of great ape, an undiscovered type of ground-dwelling orangutan, or even a surviving population of an ancient hominid
