Tokoloshe

Information

Type: Dwarf-like creature

Origin: South Africa

Threat level: EXTREME

Habitat: Water related places

Overview

The Tokoloshe (or Tikoloshe) is a mischievous, Dwarf-like water spirit, it is believed to be summoned by witches or malevolent people to cause trouble, inflict illness, or even bring death to enemies.

Appearance

Witnesses describe the Tokoloshe as a hairy, gremlin-like being. Typically described as short with coarse hair, wrinkled skin, and glowing eyes.

Habitat

Deep river pools, reed beds and riverbanks.

Reported sightings

  • There is no "first sighting" date recorded.
  • Modern sightings continue today

Theories

Beyond folklore, the Tokoloshe serves as a way to explain misfortune, unresolved crimes or illness whithin a community.
