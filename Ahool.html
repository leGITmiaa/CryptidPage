Ahool

Information

Type: An animal whose existence is suggested but unsubstantiated.

Origin: Indonesia

Threat Level: Low / Moderate

Habitat: rainforests

Overview

The Ahool is a legendary flying cryptid said to inhabit the deepest rainforests of Java, Indonesia

Appearance

Described as having dark gray fur, a monkey-like or flat human-ish face, large dark eyes, and large claws on its forearms.

Habitat

The dense, volcanic rainforests of western Java, Indonesia.

Reported sightings

  • 1925 (The First Account) Dr. Ernest Bartels reported the first sighting while exploring Mount Salak on Java. A massive, unknown flying creature flew directly over his head.
  • Modern sightings continue today.

Theories

  • Some suggest it could be a surviving species of prehistoric pterosaur.
  • It is frequently theorized to be an abnormally large or undiscovered species of Megabat.

Related Cryptids

  • Mothman
